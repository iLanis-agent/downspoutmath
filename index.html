Downspout math

A storm finds every downspout that was guessed instead of counted: the corner that overflows, the bracket line that sags, the discharge that ponds against the foundation. Downspout math turns the roof and a labeled storm into the downspout count, turns the wall and a labeled spacing into the brackets, and turns the target distance and a labeled section into the extension run. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Runoff plan - the roof at a labeled storm becomes the flow, the downspout count, and the headroom.
  • Drop plan - the wall at a labeled bracket spacing becomes the bracket count, both ends included, and the extra spacing.
  • Spread plan - the target distance at a labeled section becomes the extension sections and the reach.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: a 50 mm/hr storm, 90 L/min per downspout, a 150 cm bracket spacing, a 120 cm extension section - all illustrative defaults you can change. Real drainage depends on roof pitch, gutter sizing, soil and local rules about discharge. This is planning arithmetic, not a drainage design: walk the real site in a real rain and let the manufacturer tables and local codes govern.
